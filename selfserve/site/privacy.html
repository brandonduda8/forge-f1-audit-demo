Apex Forge Website Checkup

Privacy

Short version: we collect the website address you submit, and not much else.

What we collect

  • The website address you submit, for the free check or at checkout, and the results of checking it.
  • Payment details are handled by Square. Square collects your card or Cash App details and your email on its own checkout page. We never see or store your card number. Square's own privacy policy covers what Square does. We use the email Square gives us only to help you find a lost report link and to send the order emails described below.
  • Check history for paid orders: the date, grade, score and problems found each time we check your website, so your dashboard can show what changed.
  • For Monthly Monitoring, a short note on your Square order: when we last ran the monthly check, the month we last emailed you, and the ids of the checks that failed or couldn't be measured. It holds no personal details, and it means a server update can't make us forget your monthly check.
  • Like any web server, the server may briefly see your IP address and browser type to answer requests and limit abuse.

How long we keep it

One-time reports (Website Checkup and Pro Checkup) and their check history are deleted after 30 days. Check history is kept on the checker's server disk, which is wiped whenever the server is updated, so it may disappear sooner; it is kept on a best-effort basis. We don't keep a separate customer list: your email, payments, refunds and subscription stay with Square, and money records always come from Square.

Emails we send

Only emails about your own order, to the email you gave Square at checkout: that your report is ready (for Monthly Monitoring, with your renewal terms), for Monthly Monitoring each month's result, and, when you use Find my report, an email with your report links. Find my report sends the links to that email address instead of showing them on the page, so only someone who can read that inbox can open or cancel your order. Each email links to your dashboard, where you can manage or cancel. No newsletters, no marketing, and we never share your email.

Monthly Monitoring is only sold while these emails are switched on. While they are switched off, Find my report shows the links on the page instead, and nothing is emailed.

What the checker does to your website

The checker makes a few read-only requests to the website you submit, much like a visitor's browser. It never logs in, never submits forms and never changes anything.

What we don't do

  • No cookies. This browser remembers your last order reference (in local storage) so the report page can find your order after checkout.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No ads, and we don't sell or share your information.

Contact

There's no phone line or chat. For a problem with a paid report, use the refund button on your report page.

For a privacy question, email the support address.
