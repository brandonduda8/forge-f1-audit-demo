Local SEO check for your business website

A free automated look at the on-site basics that help local customers find and contact you. It checks your website, not your Google listing or your rankings.

Only check websites you own or are allowed to check. The free check makes three read-only requests to the site (the homepage, robots.txt and sitemap.xml) and public DNS lookups for your SPF and DMARC email records.

What it checks on your site

  • Page title (30 to 60 characters, not a generic “Home”) and meta description (120 to 160 characters).
  • One main heading (exactly one H1).
  • LocalBusiness schema — JSON-LD markup that tells search engines your business type, name, phone and address. Specific types such as Plumber, Dentist or Restaurant count.
  • A link to your Google Business Profile or Google Maps listing.
  • Phone number near the top of the page, as a tap-to-call link.
  • Hours and service area on the page.
  • On-site reviews, robots.txt and sitemap.xml, and HTTPS.

What it doesn't check

  • Your Google Business Profile itself: whether it exists, its rating or its review count.
  • Whether your name, address and phone match across directories.
  • Your search rankings, competitors or keywords.

These need data from Google or other sites, which the checker doesn't read. The report lists them as not measured.

Questions

Will this get me to the top of Google?
No one can promise that, and this doesn't. It finds on-site basics that are easy to fix and commonly missed.
What is LocalBusiness schema?
A small block of hidden code that hands search engines your business name, type, phone, address and hours in a format they read directly.
Do I need the Pro Checkup for local SEO?
Not necessarily. The Website Checkup covers your homepage in full. Pro adds title, description, heading and other basics on up to 4 more pages, such as your services or locations pages.
Does it change my site?
No. Every request is read-only.
