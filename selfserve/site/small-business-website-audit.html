An automated website audit for small businesses

Type your website and see your grade and top 3 issues in about a minute. The checker measures your live site; nothing is guessed, and anything it can't measure is listed as not measured.

Only check websites you own or are allowed to check. The free check makes three read-only requests to the site (the homepage, robots.txt and sitemap.xml) and public DNS lookups for your SPF and DMARC email records.

What the audit looks at

Six graded areas make up the score out of 100:

  • Speed (25%) — server response time, total page weight, image sizes and formats, scripts and stylesheets that block the page.
  • Mobile (20%) — the mobile viewport setting and tap-to-call phone links.
  • SEO basics (20%) — page title, meta description, one main heading, LocalBusiness schema, a link to your Google Business Profile, HTTPS, robots.txt and sitemap.xml.
  • Contact path (15%) — a phone number up top, a contact or quote form, calls to action on your pages, the path from homepage to a quote, a response-time promise, hours and service area.
  • Reviews (10%) — whether your site shows customer reviews.
  • Trust signals (10%) — about information, license and insurance mentions, real photos, social links, a guarantee or warranty, and broken links.

Two more sections get their own score and are not part of the grade: accessibility (image alt text, form labels, heading order, page language, text contrast) and security and email trust (security headers, certificate expiry, private files, SPF, DMARC and DKIM).

Free check or full report

Free check

  • Three requests: your homepage, robots.txt and sitemap.xml, plus SPF and DMARC lookups.
  • Shows your grade, score, top 3 issues, and the top accessibility and security issue.
  • Checks that need more requests (speed of linked files, inner pages, broken links, certificate expiry, private files, DKIM) are counted as not measured.

Full report

  • Everything above, measured in full: up to 40 files on your homepage, a few internal pages, your contact page and up to 25 links.
  • Every problem ranked, each with what was measured, why it matters, how to fix it and the effort.
  • A list of what passed and what couldn't be measured, and a printable PDF.

What it can't tell you

  • Core Web Vitals and how the page looks in a real browser (these need a full browser render, so they are listed as not measured).
  • Your Google Business Profile, your star ratings, and your search rankings.
  • Your traffic, ads, calls or leads.
  • Whether your contact form really delivers messages: the checker never submits forms.

Questions

How is the grade worked out?
Each check scores pass, partial or fail against a stated bar (for example, a page title of 30 to 60 characters). Checks are averaged within each area, and the six areas are weighted as shown above. Checks that couldn't be measured are left out of the score, not counted as fails.
Do I have to talk to anyone?
No. There are no calls or meetings. You run the check, pay on Square's checkout if you want the full report, and it appears on screen.
Does it change my site?
No. The checker is read-only. It never logs in, never submits forms and never changes anything.
Is this professional advice?
No. It is automated information about your website. It doesn't promise rankings, calls or sales.
