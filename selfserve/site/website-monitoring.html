Monthly website monitoring

Websites drift: a plugin update drops your phone link, a certificate stops renewing, a page loses its title. Monthly Monitoring re-runs the full checkup about once a month and shows you what changed.

Only check websites you own or are allowed to check. The free check makes three read-only requests to the site (the homepage, robots.txt and sitemap.xml) and public DNS lookups for your SPF and DMARC email records.

How it works

  1. Pay the first month on Square's checkout. Your full Website Checkup report appears on screen right away, and you get an email with your renewal terms and your dashboard link.
  2. About once a month, the checker runs the full check on your site again, automatically.
  3. Your dashboard shows each check's grade and score, and what changed since the last one: the score change, new problems and fixed problems.
  4. Each month you get one email with the result and a link to your dashboard. Monthly Monitoring is only sold while these emails are switched on.

Monthly Monitoring isn't open for sign-up right now. You can still get a one-time report from the plans on the home page.

Price and cancelling

  • Price: $29 a month. The first month includes the full report.
  • It renews monthly until you cancel, and Square charges the payment method you used.
  • Cancel online anytime from your dashboard. Your dashboard is linked from your report page, and Find my report gets it back with the email you paid with.
  • Cancelling takes effect at the end of the month you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again.

The full renewal terms are on the Terms page.

What it is not

  • It is not uptime monitoring: it doesn't watch your site minute by minute or alert you the moment it goes down.
  • It checks the same things as the full report (see the home page for the list), once a month.
  • The dashboard history is kept on the checker's server and can be lost when the server is updated; your payments and subscription are always kept by Square.

Questions

How often is my site checked?
About once a month. A site that was checked less than 25 days ago is skipped until the next run.
How do I cancel?
Open your dashboard and press Cancel monitoring, then confirm. It stays on until the end of the month you've paid for.
How much is it?
$29 a month. The first month includes the full report.
Can I monitor more than one website?
Each subscription covers the one website you enter at checkout. Start another for a second site.
Do I have to talk to anyone?
No. Sign-up, the reports and cancelling all happen online.
