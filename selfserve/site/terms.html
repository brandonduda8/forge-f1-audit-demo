Terms

These are the terms for using Apex Forge Website Checkup, run by Apex Forge in Villa Park, Illinois.

What you buy

  • Website Checkup: one automated report for the website address you enter. You pay once. It is not a subscription. Your report link works for 30 days.
  • Pro Checkup: the same report, plus up to 4 more pages of the same website checked for the basics. You pay once. It is not a subscription. Your report link works for 30 days.
  • Monthly Monitoring: a subscription for one website. You get the full report when you sign up, then the site is re-checked automatically about once a month and your dashboard shows what changed. Your report link works while the subscription is active.

The price of each plan is shown on the home page and on Square's checkout page before you pay. The price on Square's checkout is the one that applies.

Monthly Monitoring: price, renewal and cancelling

  • Monthly Monitoring is $29 a month, and the first month includes the full report. If the first month is ever priced differently from the months after it, both prices are shown here and on Square's checkout before you pay.
  • Monthly Monitoring renews automatically each month at the monthly price shown at checkout, and Square charges the payment method you used, until you cancel.
  • You can cancel online at any time from your dashboard. Your dashboard is linked from your report page, and Find my report gets it back with the email you paid with. You don't need to contact anyone to cancel.
  • Cancelling stops all future charges. Monitoring stays on until the end of the month you've already paid for, then stops.
  • Monthly Monitoring is only sold while our order emails are switched on, so you get these renewal terms and your dashboard link by email when you sign up, and Find my report can email your links again later.

What the report is

The report is automated information produced by software that measures your live website. It is not professional, legal, marketing or technical advice. Some checks can't be measured without a full browser; the report lists those as not measured instead of guessing.

No guarantees

We don't guarantee search rankings, traffic, calls, leads or sales. Results depend on many things outside the report. The service is provided as is.

Refunds

  • If the checker can't reach the website you entered, your report page offers a button that refunds you automatically.
  • If that refund is for Monthly Monitoring, we cancel the subscription first, so you won't be charged again. If the cancel can't be done at that moment, the refund waits and the page asks you to try again in a minute. Your dashboard still shows the subscription's status afterwards.
  • For any other refund request within 7 days of a payment (including a monthly renewal), email the support address and we refund that payment in full, no questions asked.
  • For any other problem with a paid report, use the buttons on your report page.

Emails

We only send emails about your own order, to the email you gave Square: that your report is ready (for Monthly Monitoring, with your renewal terms), for Monthly Monitoring each month's result, and, when you ask Find my report, your report links. Each one links to your dashboard, where you can manage or cancel. We don't send newsletters or marketing.

Check history

We keep a history of the checks we run for your order so your dashboard can show changes over time. It is kept on the checker's server and can be lost when the server is updated, so it is offered on a best-effort basis. History for one-time reports is deleted after 30 days. Your payments and subscription are always kept by Square, and what was bought, paid, refunded or cancelled always comes from Square.

Your responsibilities

Only submit websites you own or are authorized to check. Don't use the service to overload, attack or test sites you don't control.
