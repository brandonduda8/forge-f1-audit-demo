What it does not measure

Every finding is a real automated measurement of your live site. Some things need a full browser or access we don't have, so the report lists them as not measured instead of guessing:

  • Core Web Vitals (loading, layout shift and responsiveness as Google measures them in a real browser).
  • Tap-target sizes and how the page actually looks on a phone screen.
  • Your Google Business Profile, your star ratings on Google, Yelp or other sites, and your search rankings.
  • Your traffic, ads, or how many calls and leads you actually get.
  • Whether your contact form really delivers messages (we never submit your forms).
  • A full accessibility audit or a security test.

The checker reads your homepage and a small number of linked pages. It is not a full-site crawl.

Example from a fictional test site
Critical Check 2.4

Your phone number can't be tapped to call

What we measured
The homepage shows a phone number as plain text, and has 0 tap-to-call (tel:) links.
Why it matters
Visitors on a phone have to copy the number by hand to call you, and some won't bother.
How to fix it
In your site editor, turn the phone number into a phone link (most editors call this "link to phone number").
Effort
About 10 minutes.

Questions

No. There are no calls or meetings. You run the check, pay on Stripe's checkout, and your report appears on screen.

No. The checker is read-only. It makes a few polite requests to your site, the same way a visitor's browser would, and never logs in or submits forms.

No. It is automated information about your website. It doesn't guarantee rankings or sales.
