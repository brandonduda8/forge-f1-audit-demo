What the full report includes

Over 35 automated checks of your live website: six graded areas, plus accessibility and security scored on their own:

What gets checked

  • Speed — server response time, page weight, large images, scripts that block the page.
  • Mobile — mobile viewport setup and tap-to-call phone links.
  • SEO basics — page title, meta description, headings, business schema, HTTPS, robots.txt and sitemap.
  • Contact path — phone number up top, contact forms, how many clicks to reach you, hours and service area.
  • Reviews — whether your site shows customer reviews or testimonials.
  • Trust signals — about info, license and insurance mentions, image descriptions, social links, guarantees, broken links.
  • Accessibility — basic WCAG checks: image alt text, form labels, heading order, page language, and text contrast where the colours can be read from the page.
  • Security and email trust — security headers, certificate expiry, whether private files like .env are public, and the SPF, DMARC and DKIM records that stop others sending email as you.

What you get

  • A grade and score out of 100, with a score for each area.
  • Every problem ranked by how much it matters, with a plain-English fix and an effort estimate.
  • A list of what already passed.
  • A printable PDF you can hand to whoever runs your site.
  • Instant: the report appears on screen right after checkout. No account, no call, no waiting for an email.

What it does not measure

Every finding is a real automated measurement of your live site. Some things need a full browser or access we don't have, so the report lists them as not measured instead of guessing:

  • Core Web Vitals (loading, layout shift and responsiveness as Google measures them in a real browser).
  • Tap-target sizes and how the page actually looks on a phone screen.
  • Your Google Business Profile, your star ratings on Google, Yelp or other sites, and your search rankings.
  • Your traffic, ads, or how many calls and leads you actually get.
  • Whether your contact form really delivers messages (we never submit your forms).
  • A full accessibility audit (screen-reader testing, colours set in stylesheets) or a penetration test. The accessibility and security checks are a basic automated scan.

The checker reads your homepage and a small number of linked pages. It is not a full-site crawl. The Pro Checkup adds up to 4 more of your pages, each checked for the basics.

Critical Check 2.4

No tap-to-call button

What we measured
no tel: links on homepage
Why it matters
If your number is not a tap-to-call link, a phone visitor has to copy it by hand, and some won't bother.
How to fix it
Wrap the phone number in a tel: link (ideally a sticky call button on mobile) so a caller reaches you in one tap.
Effort
DIY, ~15 minutes

Questions

Do I have to talk to anyone?
No. There are no calls or meetings. You run the check, pay on Square's checkout, and your report appears on screen.
How fast is it?
A minute or two. If the checker has been idle, the first check of the hour can take up to a minute to start.
What if my site can't be reached?
If the checker can't reach your site after you pay, the report page offers a button that refunds you automatically.
Is this a subscription?
The Website Checkup and the Pro Checkup are one-time payments for one report. Monthly Monitoring is a subscription: $29 for the first month, then $19 a month. The first month includes the full report. It renews each month until you cancel.
How do I cancel Monthly Monitoring?
Open your dashboard (there's a link on your report page and in your emails, or use Find my report) and press Cancel monitoring. It stays on until the end of the month you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again.
What does the Pro Checkup add?
Up to 4 more of your pages, picked from your sitemap and menu, each checked for the basics: title, meta description, main heading, image alt text, language, mobile viewport, thin content, noindex, canonical link, and broken or redirecting pages. Pages that the time limit cut short are listed as not measured.
Do you change my site?
No. The checker is read-only. It makes a few polite requests to your site, the same way a visitor's browser would, and never logs in or submits forms. To see whether private files like .env are public, it asks for each one once without downloading it, and it reads your domain's public email records (DNS).
Can I check someone else's site?
Only check websites you own or are allowed to check.
Is this professional advice?
No. It is automated information about your website. It doesn't guarantee rankings or sales.
