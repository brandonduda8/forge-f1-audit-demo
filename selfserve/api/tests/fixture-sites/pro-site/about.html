About Sample Bakery

We bake bread every morning from flour milled in our county, and we sell it warm at the counter until it runs out. Our bakers start at four, shape each loaf by hand, and bake it on stone so the crust cracks the way it should. Besides bread we make rolls, pastries, cakes for birthdays and weddings, and a short list of seasonal pies. Orders for cakes need two days of notice, and we can deliver within ten miles of the shop for a small fee.
